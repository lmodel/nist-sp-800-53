# Glossary

* [Control family](control-family.md) - In SP 800-53, one of the twenty families the controls are sorted into, such as Access Control (AC) or Supply Chain Risk Management (SR). The schema renders a family as `Sp80053Family`, with its two letters in `family_id`.
* [Control](control.md) - In SP 800-53, a security or privacy control: a safeguard with an identifier such as AC-2, a statement, a discussion, parameters, related controls, references and enhancements. The schema renders a control as `Sp80053Control`, with its identifier in `control_identifier`; a withdrawn one keeps its identifier and says where it went.
* [Control enhancement](control-enhancement.md) - In SP 800-53, a control enhancement: added functionality or strength for a control, with an identifier such as AC-2(1), cited apart from its control. The schema renders one as `Sp80053ControlEnhancement`, with its identifier in `enhancement_id` and the control it requires in `required_controls`.
* [Organization-defined parameter](organization-defined-parameter.md) - In SP 800-53, an organization-defined parameter (ODP): a value the statement leaves to the organization, an assignment or a selection, which SP 800-53A labels such as AC-02_ODP[01]. The schema renders one as `Sp80053Parameter`, with the catalog's identifier in `parameter_id`.
* [Assessment objective](assessment-objective.md) - In SP 800-53A, an assessment objective: a determination statement an assessor checks, nested as the control statement's items are, with a label such as AC-02a.[01]. The schema renders one as `Sp80053AssessmentObjective`, with its label in `objective_label`.
* [Assessment method](assessment-method.md) - In SP 800-53A, an assessment method: examine, interview or test, applied to the documents, people or mechanisms an assessor selects from. The schema renders one as `Sp80053AssessmentMethod`, with the method in `assessment_method` and its objects in `assessment_objects`.
* [Control baseline](control-baseline.md) - In SP 800-53B, a control baseline: the controls and enhancements selected for a low, moderate or high impact system, or for privacy. The schema renders one as `Sp80053Baseline`, with its level in `baseline_level` and its selections in `baseline_controls`.
