# NIST SP 800-53 Revision 5, Release 5.2.0

NIST Special Publication (SP) 800-53, Revision 5, is titled "Security and Privacy Controls for Information Systems and Organizations". NOTICE gives its publication as September 2020, updated December 2020, and its current release as Release 5.2.0 of August 2025. It says that the publication is a work of the United States Government not subject to copyright in the United States.[^notice] The schema header carries the release as its `version` and the publication's digital object identifier as its `source`. Its annotations carry the release's date, `2025-08`, and the identifier NIST's Cybersecurity and Privacy Reference Tool gives the release, `SP_800_53_5_2_0`. Its `see_also` lists the publication page, the Release 5.2.0 announcement, SP 800-53A, SP 800-53B and the oscal-content folder.[^nist_sp_800_53.yaml] NIST's catalog of the release is titled "Electronic (OSCAL) Version of NIST SP 800-53 Rev 5.2.0 Controls and SP 800-53A Rev 5.2.0 Assessment Procedures", at version `5.2.0`.[^nist_sp-800-53_rev5_catalog.json]

The README says that Release 5.2.0 is the current release and that no later release or Revision 6 exists, and that release tags number the schema's versions.[^readme.md] `scripts/check_upstream_release.py` asks doi.org every week whether NIST has registered `10.6028/NIST.SP.800-53r6`, the identifier the next final edition would take. It reports that edition's drafts for information only. It finds a new release of Revision 5 through the catalog's blob rather than a DOI.[^check_upstream_release.py] [NIST's SP 800-53 catalog and baseline copies](../datasets/sp-800-53-catalog-copy.md) is the machine-readable form of this release that the repository keeps.
