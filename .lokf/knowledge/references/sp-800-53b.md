# NIST SP 800-53B

NIST Special Publication (SP) 800-53B defines the control baselines that select from SP 800-53's catalog. NOTICE names it beside SP 800-53 and SP 800-53A as a work of the United States Government.[^notice] NIST publishes the four baselines as OSCAL profiles beside the catalog, each importing the catalog and listing the identifiers it selects: the Low baseline's profile, at version `5.2.0`, selects 149, and the Moderate, High and Privacy profiles select 287, 370 and 96.[^nist_sp-800-53_rev5_low-baseline_profile.json]

The schema renders a baseline as `Sp80053Baseline`, with its level in `baseline_level`, one of `LOW`, `MODERATE`, `HIGH` and `PRIVACY`, and its selections in `baseline_controls`, and lists the publication's digital object identifier in `see_also`.[^nist_sp_800_53.yaml] The four [baselines](../baselines/index.md) concepts hold each selection.
