# NIST SP 800-53A Revision 5

NIST Special Publication (SP) 800-53A, Revision 5, gives the assessment procedures for the controls of SP 800-53. NOTICE names it beside SP 800-53 and SP 800-53B as a work of the United States Government.[^notice] NIST's catalog carries its content inside each active control and enhancement:

- an `assessment-objective` part with nested objectives, each labelled with the class `sp800-53a`, such as `AC-02a.[01]`;
- `assessment-method` parts whose `method` property is `EXAMINE`, `INTERVIEW` or `TEST`, each with an `assessment-objects` part.

Each organization-defined parameter but the 142 that aggregate others carries an `sp800-53a` label such as `AC-02_ODP[01]`, and 1,327 of the 1,600 carry guidelines.[^nist_sp-800-53_rev5_catalog.json]

The schema renders these as `Sp80053AssessmentObjective`, with `objective_label`, `objective_text` and `sub_objectives`, as `Sp80053AssessmentMethod`, with `assessment_method` and `assessment_objects`, and as `Sp80053Parameter`'s `assessment_label` and `parameter_guidelines`. Its header lists the publication's digital object identifier in `see_also`.[^nist_sp_800_53.yaml]
