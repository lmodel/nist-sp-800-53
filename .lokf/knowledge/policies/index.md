# Policies

* [AI covenant](ai-covenant.md) - The repository's rules for work done with artificial intelligence (AI): you own what you submit, an element under the schema renders SP 800-53, SP 800-53A or SP 800-53B in the project's own words and cites it, NIST's catalog and baseline copies are never edited, and the scheduled librarian proposes changes only through reviewed pull requests.
* [License and attribution](license-and-attribution.md) - The repository's licences: Apache 2.0 for the schema and the code, NIST's public-domain and CC0 terms for the catalog and baseline copies, NIST's statement that SP 800-53, SP 800-53A and SP 800-53B are not subject to copyright in the United States, MIT for the copier template's scaffolding, and Apache 2.0 for the KTL sidecar and website templates.
* [Security policy](security-policy.md) - How to report a vulnerability (GitHub's private vulnerability reporting), which release receives fixes (the latest), what executes in the repository and what holds each surface, the ruleset on `main`, and the two repository settings that are not yet in force.
